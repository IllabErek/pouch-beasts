Welcome trainers!

The long-awaited Pokémon championship is coming up soon. Before we get into battling, here is how everything will work and what you need to prepare.

We will be running two different tournaments, and you can join either one or both.

The tournaments

Pokémon TCG Pocket

Matches will be best of 3. Depending on how many people join, we will run a bracket that leads to a final winner.

You can build your deck however you like. Only the normal in-game restrictions apply.

Pokémon Unbound

Pokémon Unbound is one of the most popular Pokémon ROM hacks. It keeps the classic retro look but massively improves the experience and includes Pokémon up to Gen 8.

In this format, we will play what is called Single Battles. In Single Battles, each trainer sends out one Pokémon at a time to fight, with victory going to the trainer who knocks out all opposing Pokémon. A team consists of 6 Pokémon. The match structure will be similar to TCG Pocket, but team building will work a bit differently.

Team building for Unbound

You will make two teams.

Team 1 - Your own team

Build this however you want. Pick your favorites, go full meta, or try something weird. Just follow the standard competitive restrictions (more about this below).

Team 2 - Drafted team

This team will be drafted using this tool: https://illaberek.github.io/pouch-beasts

  • Roll 3 Pokémon
  • Pick 1
  • Repeat until you have 6 Pokémon

After that, you can fully customize them. Moves, abilities, builds, everything. You are also allowed (not mandatory) to evolve all your Pokémon to their last evolution stage after you chose them.

The tool also lets you plan your team and look up moves and abilities while drafting.

Rules

For a good intro to competitive Pokémon and the standard rules and restrictions, check this out: https://pokemonshowdown.com/intro

On top of that, there is one extra rule:

  • Max 1 Legendary or Mythical Pokémon per team

To keep things fair, when you have explored the Team drafter tool and are ready to draft the team you will bring to the competition, please don't cheat! :) Its all about the fun of the game and you will have your other team to live out your min/maxing urges on. ;)

When you have built your teams, make sure to send me their exported Excel sheets so I can set them up for the competition day!

You can also use Pokémon Showdown to test your teams and try different builds before the tournament.

Tournament structure

Depending on the number of participants, the tournament will be run either as a straight elimination bracket or with short group stages leading into playoffs.

The goal is to keep things fun and make sure everyone gets to play more than just a single match while ending up with one true Pokémon Master!

Prize and entry fee

The winner will take home an RG34XXSP. A faithful recreation of the Game Boy Advance SP, but with modern hardware and software capabilities.

To cover the cost of the prize, there will be a very small entry fee. The exact amount will be shared once we know the final number of participants.

Why join

  • Fun, low-pressure competitive Pokémon
  • Good excuse to theorycraft and build weird teams
  • A powerful and modern but nostalgic handheld console on the line

Practice sessions

Before the tournament, we will have informal practice sessions where you can test teams, try the draft tool, and get comfortable with the format.

These are optional but recommended, especially if you have not played competitive Pokémon before.

On the day

We will play with retro handheld consoles. One player's screen will be cast to the TV so everyone can watch the battles. There will be snacks. If you do not feel like playing, you are still very welcome to hang out and join other Pokémon-related hobbying and fun activities.

Exact dates will be discussed later and other practical info will be shared in a group chat or here. For now, enjoy drafting and learning about competitive Pokémon!
