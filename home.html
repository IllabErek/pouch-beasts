Welcome trainers!

The long-awaited Pokémon championship is coming up. Before we get into battling, here is how everything will work and what you need to prepare.

We will be running two different tournaments, and you can join either one or both.

The tournaments

Pokémon TCG Pocket

Matches will be best of 3. Depending on how many people join, we will run a bracket that leads to a final winner.

You can build your deck however you like. Only the normal in-game restrictions apply.

Pokémon Unbound

Pokémon Unbound is one of the most popular Pokémon ROM hacks. It keeps the classic retro look but massively improves the experience and includes Pokémon up to Gen 8.

The match structure will be similar, but team building works a bit differently.

Team building for Unbound

You will make two teams.

Team 1 - Your own team

Build this however you want. Pick your favorites, go full meta, or try something weird. Just follow the standard competitive restrictions.

Team 2 - Drafted team

This team will be drafted using this tool: https://illaberek.github.io/pouch-beasts

  • Roll 3 Pokémon
  • Pick 1
  • Repeat until you have 6 Pokémon

After that, you can fully customize them. Moves, abilities, builds, everything.

The tool also lets you plan your team and look up moves and abilities while drafting.

Rules

For a good intro to competitive Pokémon and the standard rules, check this out: https://pokemonshowdown.com/intro

On top of that, there is one extra rule:

  • Max 1 Legendary or Mythical Pokémon per team

You can also use Pokémon Showdown to test your teams and try different builds before the tournament.

Tournament structure

Depending on the number of participants, the tournament will be run either as a straight elimination bracket or with short group stages leading into playoffs.

The goal is to keep things competitive while making sure everyone gets to play more than just a single match.

Prize and entry fee

The winner will take home an RG34XXSP.

To cover the cost of the prize, there will be a small entry fee. The exact amount will be shared once we know the final number of participants.

Why join

  • Fun, low-pressure competitive Pokémon
  • Drafted teams keep things fresh and balanced
  • Good excuse to theorycraft and build weird teams
  • Watching matches together on the TV adds to the hype
  • Actual prize on the line

Practice sessions

Before the tournament, we will have informal practice sessions where you can test teams, try the draft tool, and get comfortable with the format.

These are optional but recommended, especially if you have not played competitive Pokémon in a while.

On the day

One player's screen will be cast to the TV so everyone can watch the battles. There will be snacks. If you do not feel like playing, you are still very welcome to hang out and join other Pokémon-related stuff.
